Questions

Common questions about painting

How do you price a paint job?

Some jobs are priced by the room and some by the whole job, depending on the work. Send a few photos or book a quick visit and we’ll give you a clear quote before anything starts.

Do you fix the walls before painting?

Yes. Holes, dents, cracks and small drywall repairs are done first, because paint over a damaged wall shows every mark.

Do you paint condos?

Yes. We work around your building’s rules for elevator bookings and work hours, and protect the floors and hallway.

Are you insured?

We’re licensed, insured and WSIB covered, and we can send the certificate to you or your property manager.

Can you do other repairs or a clean while you’re there?

Yes. We also do handyman repairs and cleaning, so ask about anything else on your list when you call.

What areas do you cover?

Toronto and across the GTA. Tell us where you are when you call or in the form.
